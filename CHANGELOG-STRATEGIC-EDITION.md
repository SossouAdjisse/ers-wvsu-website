# ERS–WVSU Strategic Edition — changes from original website

## 1. Sharper academic identity

- Repositioned ERS–WVSU as an **economics-centered, interdisciplinary research development platform**.
- Clarified that presentations from other disciplines are selected for their potential economic, quantitative, or causal-inference contribution.
- Emphasized reciprocal collaboration and preservation of disciplinary leadership.

## 2. A more credible public research profile

- Added a dedicated **Research partnerships** page with six broadly described capabilities, a four-step collaboration process, and transparent seminar-fit criteria.
- Reworked all six existing sections to use stronger research-oriented copy instead of generic interdisciplinary invitations.
- Replaced detailed project concepts with **six illustrative research themes**; no specific unannounced concepts are included.
- Referenced WVSU's announced Agriculture Complex only as independently sourced research context, with an explicit no-formal-relationship clarification.

## 3. Readability and visual design

- Main text increased to **18 px**, navigation to **15 px**, card descriptions to **16 px**, and small labels to **12 px** by default.
- Increased card headings, content contrast, controls, and spacing; retained a professional black/gold/cream identity.
- Improved responsive layouts for desktop, tablet, and phone; added a mobile navigation breakpoint before links become too cramped.
- Added **`content/appearance.json`** to adjust core font sizes and backgrounds from a single place, including in Pages CMS.

## 4. Editable architecture and safeguards

- Kept the standard-library Python generator, original SVG assets, GitHub Pages deployment, and Pages CMS editors.
- Added editable `capabilities.json`, `pathway.json`, `partnerships.json`, and `appearance.json` with corresponding CMS fields.
- Updated publication and editorial guidelines to protect project details, avoid premature partnership claims, and require approved university branding.
- Maintained no invented events, no fake registration/contact links, and no confidential research drafts.
