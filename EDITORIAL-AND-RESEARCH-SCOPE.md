# ERS–WVSU editorial policy and research boundaries

## The central idea

ERS–WVSU is an **economics-centered interdisciplinary seminar and research-development initiative**, not a general research lecture hall and not an exclusive or formally appointed advisory center.

Its value is helping colleagues across disciplines identify and strengthen the **economic reasoning, data strategy, econometric analysis, and causal-inference aspects** of their work. Researchers need not arrive with an established economic component; exploring one is part of the invitation.

## Presentation fit

A proposed talk or workshop is appropriate when at least one dimension is plausible:

- A clear economic mechanism involving incentives, behavior, markets, costs, allocation, welfare, or policy.
- A meaningful empirical-methods question involving measurement, modeling, econometrics, or data analysis.
- A research-design/identification challenge where credible causal interpretation could be developed.
- A concrete path to joint research, shared methodological development, or interdisciplinary proposal writing.

Purely general departmental briefings and unrelated promotional talks are **not** the seminar's priority. Scholars in any field may participate if their work meets the substantive fit above.

## What to reveal publicly

**Publish:** broad research themes (e.g., agriculture, health, education, labor, environmental policy), general questions (e.g., technology adoption, evaluation, distributional effects), methodological capabilities, approved event details, and completed work authorized for release.

**Keep private:** novel research questions before agreement, identification ideas, sampling designs, preliminary results, grant strategies, partner negotiations, confidential proposals, unapproved manuscripts, private data, and the three project concepts discussed while planning this site.

A public repository exposes its content files and history—even a file marked `draft` in Pages CMS. Keep sensitive items outside the public repository until explicitly approved for disclosure.

## Working with other WVSU entities

ERS–WVSU should **complement** existing scientific laboratories, departmental programs, Extension, the Economic Development Center and Sponsored Programs. Do not claim that ERS manages their activities, that a formal agreement exists, or that ERS is the only qualified research partner. Research teams retain disciplinary leadership and negotiate roles and credit together.

The planned WVSU Agriculture Complex is mentioned only as **public campus research context**, supported by an official WVSU announcement; no ERS affiliation with that construction grant is asserted.

## Name, images and institutional approvals

The black/gold palette is inspired by publicly documented WVSU colors. The E.R.S. wordmark is provisional and is **not a substitute for the WVSU-approved institutional logo**. Before using the website as an official university communication, request necessary review by WVSU Communications & Marketing and/or IT. Only post photos, graphics, recordings, and materials with appropriate rights and permission.
